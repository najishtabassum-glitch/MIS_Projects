This project focuses on analyzing sales data using Microsoft Excel to gain meaningful insights into sales performance, profitability, customer ratings, product performance, payment methods, and regional sales distribution.

The dataset was downloaded from Kaggle and processed through data cleaning, preparation, and analysis. Pivot Tables and charts were used to build an interactive Sales Dashboard that presents key business metrics and helps stakeholders understand sales trends and business performance.

🎯 Project Objectives
Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
Track total orders and quantity sold.
Understand sales performance across different product categories.
Identify sales trends over time.
Identify the top 10 best-selling products.
Analyze customer payment method preferences.
Compare profitability across product categories.
Evaluate sales performance across different regions.
Present key business insights through an Excel dashboard.

🧹 Data Cleaning & Preparation
The following data cleaning and preparation steps were performed before creating the dashboard:

Data Collection: Downloaded the raw sales dataset from Kaggle.
Duplicate Removal: Identified and removed duplicate records to improve data consistency.
Missing Value Analysis: Checked the dataset for missing values to assess data completeness.
Date Formatting: Corrected date formats to ensure consistency and support time-based analysis.
Find & Replace: Used Excel's Find & Replace feature to correct inconsistent or unwanted values.
Data Filtering: Applied filters to make the dataset easier to explore and analyze.
Data Preparation for Updates: Prepared the data structure to support future data additions and dashboard updates.
📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
The dashboard includes five key performance indicators to monitor overall business performance:

Total Sales: Measures the total revenue generated from sales.
Total Profit: Shows the total profit generated during the selected period.
Total Orders: Displays the total number of orders.
Total Quantity Sold: Measures the total quantity of products sold.
Average Rating: Shows the average customer rating, providing an overview of customer feedback.
📊 Dashboard Visualizations
The dashboard contains six charts to analyze different aspects of sales performance:

1. Sales by Category
Analyzes sales performance across different product categories to identify the categories contributing the most to total sales.

2. Sales Trend
Visualizes sales performance over time to identify trends, fluctuations, and changes in sales.

3. Top 10 Best-Selling Products
Highlights the top 10 best-selling products based on the selected sales metric, helping identify popular products.

4. Payment Method Analysis
Analyzes the distribution of orders or sales across different payment methods to understand customer payment preferences.

5. Profit by Category
Compares profitability across product categories to identify categories generating higher profits.

6. Sales by Region
Examines sales performance across different geographical regions to identify strong-performing markets and regional sales patterns.
